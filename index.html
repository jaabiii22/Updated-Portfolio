About

I design interfaces that feel effortless — grounded in user research, tested with real people, and polished down to the last pixel. I bring visual hierarchy, accessibility and design systems into every screen, logo and poster I make.

I’m a junior UI/UX designer who works in Figma with a user-centered approach. Through course and personal projects I’ve designed mobile and web interfaces, covering user research, interviews, wireframing, usability testing and high-fidelity prototypes refined with feedback.

I’m trained in the full design process, from research and information architecture to visual design and prototyping, and in WCAG 2.1 accessibility. I’m completing a BSc in Computer Science alongside a UI/UX design course, and I’m looking for an entry-level UI/UX or product design role.
